# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep SociaHive and its users safe.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **[support@sociahive.com](mailto:support@sociahive.com)** with the subject line starting with `[security]`. Please don't open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.

Include:

- What the problem is and what an attacker could do with it
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The URL, endpoint, package and version affected
- Your name or handle if you'd like to be credited

We reply within 3 business days, keep you updated while we work on a fix, and let you know when it ships. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you talk about it publicly.

## Scope

- The hosted service at [www.sociahive.com](https://www.sociahive.com), including the REST API (`/api/v1`) and OAuth endpoints
- The MCP server at `https://www.sociahive.com/api/mcp`
- The SDKs, plugins and integrations published in the [sociahive](https://github.com/sociahive) organization

Problems in Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok, X or the other platforms we connect to should go to those platforms.

## Please don't

- Run automated scanners or load tests against production
- Access, change or delete data that isn't yours. Use your own accounts, and stop as soon as you see someone else's data.
- Use phishing, social engineering or physical attacks
- Send messages or publish posts to people who didn't agree to take part

If you follow this policy in good faith, we won't take action against you for your research.
